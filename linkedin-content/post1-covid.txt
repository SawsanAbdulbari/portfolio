=== LINKEDIN POST 1: COVID-19 Prediction (Healthcare AI + Global Team) ===

I led a team of 15+ contributors across 6 countries to build a COVID-19 prediction system for Zambia.

Here's what we did and what I learned:

The problem: Developing countries lacked the infrastructure to forecast COVID-19 cases and deaths — making resource allocation nearly impossible for already strained healthcare systems.

The approach: We built an XGBoost model on imputed case/death data, evaluated on MAE, RMSE, and R². I led model evaluation and deployment while coordinating a fully remote, cross-cultural team.

The result: A live Streamlit dashboard serving public health forecasts, with all code open-sourced.

Three lessons I'd share from leading a global ML team:

1. Async communication is a superpower — when your team spans 8 time zones, written clarity beats meetings
2. Simple models with good features beat complex models with bad data — XGBoost won because it handled missingness gracefully
3. Deploy early, iterate often — the Streamlit prototype went live in week 2, not week 8

Full project: https://omdenakitwecovid19.streamlit.app/
