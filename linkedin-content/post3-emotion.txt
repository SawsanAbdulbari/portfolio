=== LINKEDIN POST 3: Emotion Recognition (Mobile Deployment + Edge AI) ===

I built an emotion recognition system that runs on a phone.

No cloud API. No GPU. Just a 4.2 MB model on a mobile device.

Here's the pipeline:

Step 1 — Model selection on FER-2013 (35,887 images, 7 emotions)
Compared ResNet, MobileNet, and EfficientNet. EfficientNet-B0 won on accuracy/size tradeoff.

Step 2 — Fine-tuning on RAF-DB (real-world, imbalanced)
Used weighted loss and augmentation to handle the class imbalance (happy is overrepresented, disgust is scarce).

Step 3 — INT8 quantization for mobile deployment
Dynamic quantization shrank the model from 16 MB to 4.2 MB with only 0.7% accuracy drop. That's the difference between "app concept" and "app that users actually download."

The full project includes the training pipeline, evaluation suite, and the quantized mobile model.
